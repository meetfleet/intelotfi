Academic research / 6 works

Research paper — September 2026 AI / SaaS billing

The Recovery Readiness Score (RRS): Calendar-Aware Scoring and Exact Retry Planning for Failed Subscription Payments

Hamza Lotfi

Research overview

When a renewal payment fails, a billing engine must decide whether to retry, exactly when, and when to stop. The Recovery Readiness Score estimates the probability of success from four interpretable factors: decline-code recoverability, alignment with the payer’s funding cycle, the rest a card has had since its last failure, and the chance the customer is still there.

Funding-cycle alignment is learned per account over day-of-month and day-of-week. A Bellman recursion turns the scores into a retry plan that maximises expected recovered revenue net of attempt costs. On 100,000 held-out synthetic invoices, RRS recovered 72.55% of failed payments versus 68.47% for a fixed ladder, with 52% fewer retries and 80% fewer wasted retries. These are synthetic-data results, not measured production outcomes.

Key contributions

  • Calendar-aware scoring

    RRS learns when in a customer’s cycle a retry is most likely to land — not just how many days to wait.

  • Exact retry planning

    A Bellman recursion plans retries to maximise expected recovered revenue net of attempt costs.

  • Four interpretable factors

    Decline recoverability, funding-cycle alignment, rest since the card’s last failure, and customer survival.

  • Honest simulation boundary

    Results are simulation-based. The paper states this clearly. Real-world performance requires real-world data.

Inventions / AI systems

AI-native decision architectures.

My inventive contribution is not in the mathematical primitives — cosine similarity, exponential decay, the Wilson bound already exist. It is in how they are assembled around a different question, and in the AI layer that gives those answers a voice.

Invention 01 — Social coordination AI

Social Activation Score (SAS)

AI-powered

SAS=Vibe alignment×Intent fit×Friction decay×Safety/density

SAS is a product, not an average. A weak factor — low safety, poor intent fit, high friction — remains visible instead of disappearing into a blended number. That structure is the invention: an explicit decision architecture for “which meetup fits these people, right now?”

A language model reads the four SAS terms and turns the dominant one into a plain sentence on the moment card (“0.91: you both love quiet cafés, 8 minutes each”). The model never scores anything itself — it calls SAS as a tool and explains what the math already decided.

  • Vibe alignment

    Cosine similarity between the merged people vibe and the venue vibe vector

  • Intent fit

    Alignment between the stated plan and the people involved

  • Exponential friction decay

    e−friction: travel, time, cost and logistics penalised continuously

  • Safety/density factor

    Safety squared combined with crowd comfort — a low-safety venue cannot be masked

AI role: A language model explains scores in plain language and drafts plans with real venues via Apple Maps. It is a voice for the math — the architecture picks, the model explains, the person decides.

Invention 02 — Trust & evidence AI

The Trust Protocol

AI-explained

T(u)=Reviews0.40×MF-input0.25×Attendance0.35×(1−risk)

The Trust Protocol combines reviews weighted by AI-estimated authenticity and recency decay, verified Meetfleet participation signals, and a Wilson lower confidence bound on show-up history. The geometric mean keeps a weak dimension visible. A risk discount reduces trust as estimated risk rises. When the lower trust score falls below 0.35, the match goes to human review — a threshold, not a verdict.

The match formula

MATCH=SAS×√T(A)×T(B)

Fit and trust are kept separate until the final ranking. A high SAS cannot conceal a low trust score; a strong trust record cannot redeem a poor plan fit.

Invention 03 — SaaS & AI infrastructure

Usage-Based AI Billing Engine

AI & SaaS infra

Any digital product — AI API, SaaS subscription, app, storage service — should charge the way it actually costs to run. Fixed monthly tiers hide real usage and create silent losses. I built the engine that solves this.

  • Prepaid usage wallets

    Wallets that top themselves up automatically from any card or digital wallet. Usage never stops and never runs at a loss.

  • Payment recovery

    Failed payments recovered automatically, often before the customer notices — guided by the Recovery Readiness Score.

  • Append-only balanced ledger

    Idempotent, duplicate-proof and zero-sum by design. A ledger that cannot lie.

  • Team seat management

    Seats bought in one tap, assigned in seconds. No team gets locked out because of one failed card.

  • Retention offers

    The right offer shown at the right moment — exactly when someone tries to cancel.

  • Seven explicit states

    Every subscription state is named. No surprises, no silent failures. Whether AI, SaaS, storage or API — billing follows usage.
