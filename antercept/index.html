The idea, in a minute

A safe rehearsal.
A useful lesson.

A convincing message can catch anyone off guard. Antercept brings the exercise and its results together, so the team can understand what happened.

  1. 01 / PREPARE

    Choose a realistic message.

    A delivery update, a workspace invitation, or a payroll notice. Give the exercise a clear learning goal.

  2. 02 / TRY

    See it from the recipient’s side.

    Would they open the link or report the message? In this demo, you make that choice on the phone.

  3. 03 / LEARN

    Explain the result.

    Review the actions taken and the clues in the message. Use them to guide the follow-up conversation.

A useful result explains the behaviour: the message created urgency, the recipient followed its link, or they recognised something suspicious and reported it.

The proposed AI assistant would help draft reviewed training messages and summarise these patterns. This demo uses written explanations, not a live AI model. A click alone cannot tell you whether a device is infected.
