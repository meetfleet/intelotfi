The idea, in a minute

A leak is a starting point.
You need the next step.

A list of stolen data can be hard to act on. Breachify connects each finding to an account, explains the concern, and keeps the evidence close.

  1. 01 / FIND

    Which accounts are exposed?

    Look up a company’s domain—the address after the @ in its work email.

  2. 02 / UNDERSTAND

    What could someone access?

    See whether the record contains a password, a saved sign-in, or signs of an affected computer.

  3. 03 / RESPOND

    What should the team review?

    Prepare a clear list of follow-up actions, with the supporting records attached.

Less decoding. More understanding.

The result should
explain itself.

“A session cookie was exposed” means little on its own. “A saved sign-in may let someone access this account without typing its password” tells you why to pay attention.

That’s the role of the proposed AI assistant: turn technical records into a short explanation, point to the evidence, and state what is still unknown. The demo uses written sample explanations to show that experience.
