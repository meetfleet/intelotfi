AI · data science · risk assessment

From a raw log
to a reasoned decision.

A hybrid intelligence architecture: structured evidence, entity correlation, explainable ranking, and an AI analyst that cites its sources.

A score is only useful when you can question it. Change the evidence below, inspect each contribution, and follow the possible attack path. Unknown inputs produce a range; the analyst brief distinguishes observations from hypotheses. This is the proposed intelligence layer, demonstrated with an explicit local model.
