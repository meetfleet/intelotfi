Evidence design

When a target clicks a simulated lure, the test has already taught its lesson. What Antercept does next is turn that moment into clear evidence of exposure — the artefact a security team hands to leadership.

A click, as evidence

In an authorized awareness test, an interaction yields a session marker, a cookie, or a submitted credential. Antercept records each as evidence against the target's record, with the time and the source, so the exposure is measurable rather than anecdotal.

Reading the console

The operator console on the project page shows this directly: open a target to see its device, its city, the evidence its interaction produced, and a risk score that rises with exposure and resets when the test is resolved.

Open the live console →

Resolving the test

Evidence is only useful if it leads somewhere. Each target can be marked resolved once the lesson is delivered, moving it to a reported state and closing the loop — the measure of a successful awareness programme is how quickly that happens.

ⓘ
About the data

All sessions, cookies, credentials and locations referenced here are fictional sample data. Nothing is captured from a real person, and the console performs no real action.
