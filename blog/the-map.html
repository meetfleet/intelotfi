Operations

A single awareness email is easy. Running a programme — many campaigns, many audiences, over months — is an infrastructure problem. Antercept's third job is to manage that machinery so the security team can focus on the result.

The map

Geolocation and IP views place each interaction on a map: where a target opened a message, where a click came from, which office or region is most exposed. For an awareness programme, the map is a heat chart of where to train next.

The machinery

Behind the map sit the servers that host the test pages, the domains that carry the campaign, and the providers that deliver email and SMS. Antercept manages them as one inventory, so a campaign can be stood up, run, and torn down cleanly.

One console, end to end

From the first template to the final report, every module — Phisher, Cookies, Credentials, Locater, IPs, Extracter, Data, Templates, Servers, Domains, Users and Settings — lives in one console. That is what makes Antercept a platform rather than a tool.

About the data
